## Overview

My first year with UVA Rocketry was on the air brakes team for the International Rocket
Engineering Competition. IREC rewards rockets for peaking close to a target altitude,
and air brakes are how you get there: flaps that deploy from the body during flight to
add drag and bleed off just enough energy that the rocket tops out where it is
supposed to.

## From CAD to Hardware

My work on the system ran from design through integration. I designed parts for it in
SOLIDWORKS, manufactured the carbon fiber flaps, and then helped assemble the full air
brakes system and integrate it into the rocket.
