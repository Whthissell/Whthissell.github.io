## Overview

UVA Rocketry competes in the International Rocket Engineering Competition (IREC), and
this fall I started leading a sub-team inside the club's R&D group. Our job is the fuel
injector for a hybrid rocket engine. The injector controls how propellant flows into
the combustion chamber, so getting it right matters for the whole engine.

## Designing Around a Flow Coefficient

The design starts from a target flow coefficient. I built the injector's 3D model in
CAD to meet that target, then used computational fluid dynamics to understand how the
fluid actually moves through the geometry.

## Leading the Sub-Team

I direct the sub-team's design work, and a good part of the job is coordination. The
injector has to integrate with the hardware around it, so I work with the bulkhead and
valve sub-teams to make sure our pieces fit together properly.
